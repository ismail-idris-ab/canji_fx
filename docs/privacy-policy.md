# Aboki Rate privacy policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Aboki Rate shows Nigerian foreign-exchange rates. It is an information service: it does not buy or sell currency, does not connect you to anyone who does, and never handles your money.

This policy says exactly what Aboki Rate collects. The short version is that it collects very little, and none of it identifies you.

## What Aboki Rate does not do

- It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
- It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
- It does not use advertising, analytics or tracking software of any kind.
- It does not sell or share anything with third parties for marketing.
- It does not know your location.
- It does not read your contacts, photos, files or messages.

## What Aboki Rate does collect

### An anonymous device identity

When you first open Aboki Rate, it creates an anonymous account so the app can store settings that belong to your device. This account contains no personal information — it is a random identifier and nothing else. You are never asked for details, and none are inferred.

If you never set a rate alert, this identifier is deleted automatically after 30 days of inactivity.

### A notification token, only if you set a rate alert

If you create a rate alert, Aboki Rate asks permission to send notifications. If you agree, your device gives Aboki Rate a notification token so a message can reach it. The token is stored with your alert and is used for nothing else.

The token identifies a device, not a person. It is shared only with Expo's push notification service, which delivers the message. If you uninstall the app, the token stops working and Aboki Rate removes the alerts attached to it.

You can use every other part of Aboki Rate without granting notification permission, and nothing asks for it until you create your first alert.

### Your rate alerts

If you create an alert, Aboki Rate stores the currency, the market, the direction and the threshold you chose, so it can tell you when the rate crosses it. You can delete any alert at any time, which deletes that record.

### Rates saved on your device

Aboki Rate saves the last rates it fetched to your own device, so the app still shows something when your connection is poor. This never leaves your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Where the data is kept

Aboki Rate's data is stored with Supabase, on servers in London, United Kingdom. This means the small amount of data described above is stored outside Nigeria.

Notification tokens are additionally handled by Expo's push service in order to deliver a notification to your device.

## Where the rates come from

Official market rates are published by the [Central Bank of Nigeria](https://www.cbn.gov.ng) and are reproduced without alteration. Parallel market rates come from market observation and are indicative — they describe what was seen, not a price anyone is obliged to honour.

News items are headlines and links to articles published by other organisations. Tapping one opens that publisher's own website in your browser, where their privacy policy applies, not this one. Aboki Rate does not store article text.

## Removing your data

Delete your alerts inside the app to remove them and the notification token attached to them.

Uninstalling the app removes the saved rates from your device. The anonymous identifier is deleted automatically after 30 days once no alerts remain.

If you would like anything removed sooner, contact us at the address below. Because Aboki Rate holds no personal information, we may need you to tell us which device identifier to remove.

## Children

Aboki Rate is not directed at children and collects nothing that would identify anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app.
